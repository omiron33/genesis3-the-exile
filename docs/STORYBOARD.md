| 0:00.0–0:10.9 | Title. Black, then in the first second a low fast glide over a river at golden hour, mist on the water, reeds and trees rushing past; the camera rises and the garden of Delight opens ahead: hills of orchards, four rivers shining, a huge tree on a rise in the far centre. GENESIS 3 / THE EXILE sets in tall light capitals and dissolves into the haze. | Paradise is real and we are in it; the first second is already moving. | The camera keeps rising into a wide over the meadow. | Gold light, the river, the tree on the rise far off. |
