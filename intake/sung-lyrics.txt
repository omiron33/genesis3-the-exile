Among the creatures the Lord God formed,
none was as sly as the serpent.
He turned to the woman:
Did God close every garden tree to you?
She answered him: We eat the fruit
from trees throughout the garden.
Only the tree at its center is barred.
God told us not to eat its fruit,
not even to touch it,
lest we die.
The serpent replied:
No death will come from this.
God knows the day you taste it,
your eyes will come awake.
Like gods you will discern
what is good and what is evil.
She saw food in its branches,
beauty before her eyes,
something fair to ponder.
She plucked and ate the fruit,
passed it to her husband there beside her;
he ate as well.
Then both could see, and knew
their nakedness. They joined
the leaves of figs by hand
and wrapped themselves.
That afternoon they heard His voice,
the Lord God walking through the garden.
Adam and his wife turned from His face
and hid within the trees.
He called out to Adam:
Adam, where are you now?
I heard You walking in the garden;
naked, I was afraid,
and hid.
Who told you you were naked?
Did you take and eat
from the tree I alone forbade?
Adam said: The woman You gave
to live beside me handed me
its fruit; I ate.
To the woman God said:
What have you done?
The serpent tricked me, she replied;
I ate.
God said to the serpent:
Because of this, the curse falls on you
above the herd and every beast of earth.
Your breast and belly shall bear you;
earth will be your food
through all your days.
I will make enmity stand
between you and the woman,
your seed and hers.
He will keep watch against your head,
and you against his heel.
To the woman: Your pain and groaning
I will multiply. In pain
you will bring children forth.
To your husband you will submit;
he will hold rule over you.
To Adam: You heard your wife
and ate from the tree I marked
as the one you must not eat.
The earth is cursed under your work;
with pain you will live from it
all your days.
It will give you thorn and thistle;
the herbs of the field
will be your food.
Your face will sweat for bread
till you go back to the earth
from which you came. For you are earth;
to earth you will return.
Adam gave his wife the name Life,
for she was mother of all who live.
The Lord God made garments of skin
for Adam and his wife
and clothed them.
Then God said: Adam is now
as one of us in knowing
good from evil. Lest he reach
for the tree of life, eat,
and live forever.
So the Lord God sent him
from the garden of Delight to work
the earth from which he came.
He drove Adam out and settled him
across from Delight's garden.
He stationed cherubs there,
and the turning sword of fire
to keep the path
to the tree of life.
